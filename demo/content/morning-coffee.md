# It's Not Just About the Caffeine

There is something grounding about the ritual of making coffee. The sound of the beans grinding, the smell of the grounds, the slow pour of the water.

It is a few minutes of mindfulness before the chaos of the day begins.

## My Current Setup

I've been experimenting with a few different methods lately:

*   **V60:** For when I want a clean, tea-like cup.
*   **Aeropress:** For a quick, strong brew.
*   **French Press:** For lazy Sunday mornings.
